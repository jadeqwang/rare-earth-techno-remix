COMPATIBILITY AND INSTALL
The pack targets both StepMania 3.9 and StepMania 5. StepMania 3.9 reads the
SM file; StepMania 5 reads the SSC file and also supports SM. Both exports
contain all eight charts, with identical notes and shared timing.

Extract Rare_Earth_DDR_Step_Pack.zip into your game's Songs directory.
The resulting layout should be:
  Songs/Rare Earth Pack/Rare Earth (Techno Remix)/
    Rare Earth (Techno Remix).ssc
    Rare Earth (Techno Remix).sm
    Rare_Earth_DDR.mp3
    banner.png
    background.jpg
    jacket.png
    README.txt

Reload songs or restart the game, then select Rare Earth Pack. If replacing
the first edition, replace the existing song folder and reload the song cache.

DESIGN
Beginner: mostly two-beat spacing, a few quarter-note accents, easy freezes;
          no jumps or eighth notes. Double travels more slowly between pads.
Easy: quarter-note patterns, four jumps and phrase-end freezes; no eighths.
Standard: quarters, short eighth-note phrases, jumps on strong accents,
          lighter transitions and freezes.
Heavy: longer eighth-note streams, brief sixteenth-note bursts in the build
       and drops, jumps, and phrase-end breathing room. Double keeps rapid
       sixteenth bursts on one pad, with travel during surrounding phrases.

All charts start at 3.7745 seconds, allowing time to see the opening arrows.
The tempo follows the existing instrumental beat analysis with one BPM
segment per measure. Musical rests remain rests; no added stops, speed
gimmicks, mines, rolls, lifts, or three-panel combinations are used.
The first edition's three singles and Standard Double retain their steps;
only their Intermediate/Advanced labels became Standard/Heavy.
Audio, timing, banner, jacket, and background remain the same.

VERIFICATION
Both exports were parsed strictly with simfile 2.1.1. Checked all four levels
in each mode, equivalent note data, hold pairing, notes within the audio,
all panels used, timing at every beat, original audio checksum, and a valid
alternating two-foot path with limited stance width and double travel.
Difficulty meters and step counts increase through the four levels.
The previews are autoplay renderings of the exported SSC, not recordings
from a launched game. No physical pad playtest or game-engine launch was
performed for either version.

Environment design sheet for the Earth locations of an anime sci-fi music video set in San Francisco and at a radio observatory. Style: premium anime background art (Makoto Shinkai light, clean line, flat graphic color), crisp and luminous, no people.
Panels with small clean captions:
1) ABOVE THE FOG — night, a tall red-and-white three-pronged TV antenna tower (like San Francisco's Sutro Tower) rising out of a thick rolling sea of fog, blinking red aviation lights, city glow bleeding orange through the fog, stars above, a small maintenance platform near the top.
2) THE ARRAY — a high-desert valley at night with forty-two white six-meter radio dishes on pedestals in a loose grid, all pointed at the same patch of the Milky Way, volcanic mountain silhouette behind.
3) NIGHT SHIFT — a cozy small radio-observatory control room at 3 a.m.: dark room, several monitors showing blue waterfall spectrograms and a single bright vertical line, a desk with coffee, headphones, sticky notes, a window showing a dish outside under stars.
4) LAUNCH — a giant stainless-steel rocket on a launch tower with huge mechanical catch arms at a coastal launch site at blue hour, steam venting.
5) PALE BLUE DOT — a vertical beam of scattered sunlight in black space with one tiny pale blue speck inside it.
